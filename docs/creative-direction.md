# Wedfolio — creative direction for template intros

Rules for every intro
- Full-screen from the first frame (no small object floating in empty space). Rich, lit materials: pearl/iridescence, satin/velvet sheen, glass, paper grain.
- The couple's names (and ideally the date) appear inside the 3D/interactive moment, not on a page laid over it.
- One gesture from the guest (tap, swipe, drag, hold). 3–6 s total, skippable, reduced-motion → skipped.
- The last frame hands off into the page hero (no hard cut).
- Everything is procedural: three.js geometry/shaders/particles, canvas-drawn textures, SVG. No photos.
- Fonts must cover Turkish (ğ ş ı İ).

## Existing templates — new intros
1. Celestial — "Make a wish": starry sky; tap/swipe sends a shooting star whose dust writes the names; date appears; warp through the stars.
2. Art Deco — "The emerald cut": emerald on black lacquer with gold pinstripes; drag to rotate; facets flash, sunburst of gold rays, stepped Deco frame extrudes; monogram refracted in the gem.
3. Sunny Storybook — "Pull the tab": closed inked storybook cover with a red pull tab; cover opens, paper layers pop up (trulli, lemon tree, Vespa, doorway); a paper bird carries the names on a ribbon.
4. Sakura — "Shoji at dusk": backlit shoji screens with a swaying branch shadow; swipe them apart; a gust of petals swirls and settles into the names.
5. Autumn Falls — "Catch the leaf": one maple leaf tumbling; tap to catch; it turns face-on, an ember edge burns the names into the leaf; wind sweeps many leaves through.
6. Ege Zeytin — "Knock twice": Aegean-blue door in a whitewashed wall under bougainvillea; knock; door swings in, sunlight floods, courtyard and sea; names on a blue ceramic house plate.
7. Sole & Mare — "Paint the coast": blank watercolour paper; touch; pigment blooms into sea, cliff houses, sky; names hand-lettered in paint.
8. Royal Garden — "The rose opens": deep-rose bud on black velvet by candlelight; press and hold; petals unfurl; gold-foil monogram in the heart.
